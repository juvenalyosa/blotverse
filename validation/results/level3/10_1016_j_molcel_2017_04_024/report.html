About this report

Every defensible combination of analysis choices listed in the frozen specification was run; all of them are shown. BlotVerse never selects or recommends a single universe. Verdict thresholds: Robust if R ≥ 0.9 and joint p < 0.05; Fragile if R ≥ 0.5; otherwise Not supported. Not evaluable means the design has too few lanes per blot for the joint test to ever reach its alpha. The reproducibility file repro.yaml regenerates these numbers exactly on the same platform and compiler.
