## Panels examined

None: no panel fits the unit of analysis (target band normalised to a loading control and
compared between groups). The deposited LI-COR files (`.scn`, `.gel`, 700/800 TIF) belong to the
unquantified Fig. 1C/F/G knockout-confirmation blots and to TRAP gels.

## Decision

**Excluded, criterion 1**: no western blot panel quantified by the authors. No `fetch.sh` or
`claim.yaml` written. `images/` and `archives/` are left as downloaded by `screen_files.py`; the
xlsx quantification files of the deposit remain inside `archives/Chen Fig.1.zip` and were not
extracted or opened (they belong to the TRAP assays per the deposit description).
